### About

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering student at [North South University](https://www.northsouth.edu/) who likes finding creative fixes for everyday problems. When something on my PC, TV or phone annoys me, I usually end up writing an app for it. Outside code, I'm into video editing, content creation, audio production and running Discord communities.
