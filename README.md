## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hey! I'm **S M Tasin Uddin**, a **Computer Science & Engineering** undergraduate at **North South University**, based in **Dhaka, Bangladesh** 🇧🇩.

I'm a tech enthusiast who likes poking at technology, trying out ideas, and finding creative fixes for everyday problems. When something on my PC, TV or phone annoys me, I usually end up writing an app for it. That's how most of the repos below started.

> I believe programming is more than writing code. It's about understanding the problem, thinking it through, and building something that makes life a little easier.
